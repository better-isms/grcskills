# Access control policy

Version 1.0. Owner: Head of Engineering (role vacant since the last reorganisation).

- Every account uses single sign-on with multi-factor authentication.
- Production access is granted through a ticket approved by the team lead.
- Team leads review who has access to production and to the billing system every quarter and keep a record of the review.
- Leavers lose all access on their last working day.
- Shared accounts are not allowed.

Exceptions: the legacy reporting server uses a shared admin login until it is retired.
