# Information security policy

Version 1.0. Owner: Head of Engineering. Approved by the CEO at the founding board meeting. Next review: 12 months after approval.

## Purpose

Quillfield protects the confidentiality, integrity and availability of customer invoice data and of our own systems.

## Scope

All employees, contractors, systems and data of Quillfield Software.

## Commitments

- We keep an up-to-date risk register and review it every quarter.
- All staff complete security awareness training at onboarding and every year.
- Access to production is limited to engineers on the on-call rota.
- Customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
- Security incidents are reported to the Head of Engineering within 24 hours.

## Roles

The Head of Engineering owns this policy and the security programme. Each team lead is responsible for applying it in their team.
