## Fixes applied

1. Samples tied to booked time: a sample below the rule of thumb is written with its reason as a scope limitation for the report.
2. Management-loop tests added for a management system certification: objectives defined and measured, this audit planned in the programme, findings routed to corrective action with a root cause, results reaching management review.
3. Intake asks who the auditor is and whether they own or run part of the process; any involvement and compensating review is recorded at the top of the checklist.
4. One or two re-performance tests per area proposed for the auditor to run with the owner told first; the agent never runs them.
5. The checklist ends with a blank summary block (tests performed, passed, failed, scope limitations, findings with grade and owner, conformity to own documents) that becomes the audit report.
