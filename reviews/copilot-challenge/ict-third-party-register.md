## Fixes applied

1. The register is a standing deliverable: DORA has applied since 17 January 2025; Art. 28(3) asks for at least yearly reporting on new arrangements and the full register on request; a stale register is a finding in itself.
2. The concentration page is named as input to the entity's concentration risk assessment (Art. 29): substitutability, several arrangements with the same or closely connected providers, third-country subcontracting.
3. Exit check scoped to critical or important functions with a documented, tested, periodically reviewed exit strategy (Art. 28(8)); other arrangements need only an exit note.
4. A contract silent on subcontracting conditions is a contract gap for legal review (Art. 30(2)(a)), not just an unknown.
5. The register owner role, cadence and change route are drafted as a line for the ICT third-party risk strategy and policy owner (Art. 28(2)), where one exists.

## Fixes declined

- None. Copilot's fix 4 said Art. 30 requires notification of intended subcontracting; the text verified puts subcontracting conditions in Art. 30(2)(a) (detail in the RTS under Art. 30(5)), so the skill cites 30(2)(a) and does not claim a notification duty.
