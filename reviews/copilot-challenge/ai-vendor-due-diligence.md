## Fixes applied

1. Processing agreement content check: the GDPR Art. 28(3) terms in plain words, not just existence of a DPA.
2. Subprocessor change notice with a right to object (GDPR Art. 28(2)), flagged as the clause that matters most for AI vendors.
3. Deep tier: EU declaration of conformity, CE marking and registration (EU AI Act Art. 47 to 49).
4. Model changes: notice of changes affecting performance; a vendor change can shift the company's AI Act position if it fine-tunes or rebrands.
5. Result recorded in the supplier register (tier, conditions, re-review date).
