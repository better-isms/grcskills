## Fixes applied

1. First certification: plan the internal audit round and the management review a full quarter before the main certification audit, so corrective actions show evidence of effect.
2. When several management system standards are in scope, each one's own areas (such as privacy risk assessment or AI impact assessment) become audit areas; the coverage matrix has one column per framework.
3. A consultant's readiness review is a separate deliverable and does not replace the internal audit.
4. Each auditor's competence carries a pointer to the record that shows it; the certification auditor samples that trail.
5. The management system processes (scope, leadership commitment, objectives, competence, document control, internal audit, corrective action, management review) get a fixed set of rows in the coverage matrix.
