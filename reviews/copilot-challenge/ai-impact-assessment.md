## Fixes applied

1. Step 1: a fundamental rights impact assessment is done before first use and notified to the market surveillance authority on the official template.
2. Step 1: expect a GDPR data protection impact assessment when the system evaluates people with legal or similarly significant effects (GDPR Art. 35(3)(a)).
3. Step 3: evidence carries provenance (test set, date, who ran it); bias testing that needs special category data checks the amended AI Act's conditions first.
4. Step 4: what happens when a metric trips: deployer informs the provider and suspends use where warranted (Art. 26(5)).
5. Step 5: name where the signed record is kept and send it to the next management review.

## Fixes declined

- "Art. 4a" for bias-detection processing of special category data: article number not verified against the amended text (EUR-Lex fetch blocked); kept as "the conditions the amended AI Act sets", no number.
- Post-market monitoring Art. 72 link: provider duty, not needed for a deployer-side assessment; skipped for length.
- ISO 42001 clause and Annex A identifiers: not copied (IP rule).
